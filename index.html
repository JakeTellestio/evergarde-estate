The estate

Warm stone, quiet gardens, mountain light

Set on Main Road in Hogsback, Evergarde is a substantial stone manor with a pool courtyard, fenced gardens, and private walkways — built for lingering weekends and longer escapes.

Book the main house or upper floor on Airbnb, stay in Rose Cottage on the grounds, or enquire about forthcoming estate experiences — from a coffee shop and plant nursery to an art gallery and a new cottage.
